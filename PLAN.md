## 1. What the website has to prove (grading lens)

Per `Week 1 Dag 2A Practicalities`:

- **L1 Signaleren** — signals from the world captured (Tegenlicht, Hunt, stoepwatch, scanplan, Eva's trends)
- **L2 Analyseren** — signals ordered & analysed (trendpyramides, assenstelsel, DESTEP, scenario's)
- **L3 Waardeverschuiving & 1–3 kansrijke ideeën** — deep analysis + what it means for my business (cesuur!)
- **L4 Delen** — original, creative, valuable communication of the above (cesuur!) → **this website**

Rule of thumb: every chapter must visibly carry at least one learning outcome, and L3/L4 must be
unmistakable.

## 2. Narrative arc (one line)

> "I started with *who I am*, opened my senses to *what the world is doing*, made *sense* of it,
> found *what is shifting*, and that points exactly at *the company I'm building*:
> **helping small companies take back control of their tech (open source, Linux, local AI, automation)**."

The arc is a journey (matches the minor's name: Ontdekking**sreis** / trend**reis** — "Kom mee op reis").

## 4. 3D anaglyph concept (creative signature)

**Effect**: red-cyan anaglyph rendering of key scenes — **designed for actual red-cyan 3D glasses**
(3D is the primary mode, not a gimmick). Design rules:
- **Text stays flat** (no depth on readable copy) so it remains legible through the glasses; only
  graphics/scenes get depth
- Keep red/cyan tinting clean; avoid large magenta-ish text areas
- **Depth slider** (0 = flat → max = strong stereo) + 3D on/off toggle
- Optional: side-by-side / cross-eye mode + "explode" (pyramid layers separate)

**Key 3D scenes** (each also works as clean 2D fallback):
1. **Trendpyramide** — 4 layers (product/markt/consument/maatschappij) with his examples on each; explode to read levels
2. **Dots-constellation** — nodes = signals → trends → waardeverschuivingen → **his business idea as the central star**; edges = the connections ("connecting the dots" made literal, in 3D space)
3. **Levenswiel** — 3D radar with his scores
4. **Ikigai** — 4 circles as a 3D Venn
5. **Waardeverschuiving** — "was/is" pair as a 3D bridge (e.g. auto → … → intelligente outsourcing)

**Design language**: journey/compass motif, notebook-paper feel for content (his handwriting photos as
authentic artifacts), one bold accent colour, print-friendly fallback. (Details in a later design pass.)
